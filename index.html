SwRGlobal Radio

Tune into real radio receivers around the world - live, from your phone. SwR connects to hundreds of receivers across five clients - KiwiSDR/Web-888, OpenWebRX, UberSDR, FM-DX and AirSpy - and turns the ionosphere into your personal listening room.

01

Live spectrum & waterfall

Watch the whole band breathe in real time. A glowing frequency display, an authentic S-meter and a full waterfall view make hunting for signals feel like the real thing.

02

Different types of waterfalls & spectrums

Switch the waterfall into a rolling 3D landscape or a hypnotic 4D warp. Radio spectrum has never looked this good on a phone.

03

A worldwide receiver network

Find your receiver anywhere in the world and choose from 5 clients: KiwiSDR/Web-888, OpenWebRX, UberSDR, FM-DX and AirSpy. Watch where the sun is shining and where it's already night.

04

Shape the sound

A built-in equalizer, adjustable bandwidth, noise reduction and smart scanning. Fine-tune every station until it sounds exactly right.

05

Pixel mode

A retro monochrome skin that turns the whole receiver into a blocky pixel display. Frequency, spectrum and waterfall - reimagined as glowing dot-matrix art.

06

FM-DX

FM broadcast radio, right on your phone (87.5-108 MHz). Tuning by hand is fast and lands right on frequency, so drag the dial to hunt for stations.

07

Widgets

Two types for KiwiSDR and FM-DX. Browse the web or do anything else on your phone while the radio keeps playing - and retune any time straight from the widget.

08

Your own USB dongle

Plug in an SDR dongle and listen completely offline - no internet, no server, no limits. Just your antenna and the whole band.

09

Satellite tracking and listening

Catch transmissions from orbit right on your phone. Live satellite positions, real passes over your head - and every receiver that can hear them.

10

Plane tracking and listening

Follow air traffic anywhere in the world. Live aircraft on the map with flight, type, altitude and speed - plus the nearest airport and its tower frequency, so you can tune in and hear the pilots.

11

Free version

Enjoy listening to stations around the world completely free and without ads.

02 · SwR family · Apple Watch

SwR WatchShortwave on your wrist

A real shortwave radio on your Apple Watch. SwR Watch connects to public KiwiSDR and Web-888 receivers around the world - tune them with the Digital Crown, watch the band and listen, right on your wrist. A standalone watch app: no iPhone, no account, no sign-up.

Download on theApp Store

One-time purchase Pay once, keep it forever - no subscription.

Apple Watch only Standalone No account

AM, LSB, USB, CW and NBFM with adjustable bandwidth, volume and squelch. Audio keeps playing with your wrist down, and if a receiver drops you, SwR Watch reconnects or moves to the next best one. A separate app from SwR - Global Radio, with the same spectrum and waterfall look.

03 · New from Kruen

CmACrypto Market Analysis

Every coin traded on Binance gets a single score from 0 to 100 % - and the app shows you exactly where that number comes from. Fourteen metrics across market, price, supply, fundamentals, development, community and risk, each with its own weight, all one tap away.

Get it onGoogle Play Coming soon to theApp Store
No ads Zero data collection

A metric with no data is left out of the maths instead of being guessed, so "we don't know" never moves the score up or down. Prices come live from Binance, everything else is pre-computed and served from our own gateway. It is an orientation tool built from public data - not investment advice.

PBHPhone Battery Health

How much of its factory capacity has your battery actually lost? PBH reads the fuel gauge chip directly - charge counter, voltage, temperature - estimates the real full capacity in mAh and follows it over time.
